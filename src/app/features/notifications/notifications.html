Integration Guide

You can push real-time summaries and update reports directly to your team's admin channels by linking a Discord Webhook.

How to gather a Discord URL:
  1. Open Discord and head to your designated server settings page.
  2. Navigate to the Integrations submenu and click Webhooks.
  3. Create a new loader hook and assign it to your notification channel.
  4. Click Copy Webhook URL and paste it directly into the input form!
Pro-Tip: Disabling the master toggle blocks all background task notification pushes immediately without deleting your webhook URL!
